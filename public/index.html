About Uma

Uma is a personal AI assistant built to answer clearly, stream responses in real-time, and keep your conversations available in local history.

Ask about Java, Python, REST APIs, portfolio ideas, system debugging, project text, interview prep, or explore any concept step by step.

How History Works

Guest chats are stored locally in your browser. When signed in, your conversations securely sync to your account database in the cloud so you can access them across your devices. Use New conversation to start fresh, or Clear to wipe stored history.

Privacy

Messages are processed via Uma's backend services with real-time streaming. Your data is isolated to your private account and never shared.
