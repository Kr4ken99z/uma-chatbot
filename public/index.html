About Uma

Uma is a personal AI assistant built to answer clearly, stream responses in real-time, and keep your conversations available in local history.

Ask about Java, Python, REST APIs, portfolio ideas, system debugging, project text, interview prep, or explore any concept step by step.

How History Works

Your chat history is saved right here in your browser through local storage. Use New conversation to start fresh, or Clear to wipe stored history.

Privacy

Messages are processed via Uma's backend services with streaming support. Your sidebar history remains locally on your device unless cleared.
